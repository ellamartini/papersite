In an effort to satisfy these male audiences, nearly all characters in Yakuza games are men. Women are largely relegated to supporting roles where they provide emotional and/or sexual gratification to the male protagonist/player as he punches his way through the yakuza underworld. Female characters are most commonly cast as cabaret hostesses, where they mix drinks, sensually light cigarettes, and engage male patrons in flirty conversation. The male protagonist/player who enters this space not only enjoys an opportunity to decompress from the stresses of his daily life, but also reinforces his gender identity by participating in a dynamic where maleness itself grants him the attention of beautiful women.
